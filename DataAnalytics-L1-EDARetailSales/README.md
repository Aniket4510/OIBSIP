Retail Sales Analysis

 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of retail sales data using Python and Pandas. The analysis focuses on understanding sales trends, customer demographics, product performance, and purchasing behavior.

The project was completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship (OIBSIP)**.

 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall retail sales performance.
* Identify monthly and quarterly sales trends.
* Analyze customer demographics such as age and gender.
* Identify high-performing product categories.
* Analyze customer spending patterns.
* Study relationships between numerical variables using correlation analysis.
* Generate meaningful business insights and recommendations.

 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses a retail sales dataset containing information about:

* Transaction ID
* Date
* Customer ID
* Gender
* Age
* Product Category
* Quantity

  📊 Key Findings

  Q4 recorded the highest quarterly sales of 126,190.**
   Q3 recorded the lowest quarterly sales of 96,045.**
   Clothing had the highest quantity sold with 894 units.**
   Electronics generated the highest revenue of 156,905.**
    Customers aged **18–24 had the highest average transaction amount of approximately 501.01.**
   Customers aged **55–64 had the lowest average transaction amount of approximately 417.55.**
   rice per Unit and Total Amount showed a strong positive correlation of approximately 0.852.**

💡 Important Business Insight

Although **Clothing had the highest quantity sold**, **Electronics generated the highest revenue**. This shows that the category with the highest sales volume is not necessarily the category generating the highest revenue.

💼 Business Recommendations

1. The business should closely monitor Electronics inventory and identify the products contributing most to its high revenue.

2. Since Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold, the business should maintain adequate stock and identify the most popular Clothing products.

3. Customers aged 18–24 had the highest average transaction amount. The business could analyze their purchasing patterns and develop targeted offers based on their preferences.

4. Since Price per Unit showed a strong positive relationship with Total Amount, the business should carefully monitor pricing and product mix when evaluating transaction value.
